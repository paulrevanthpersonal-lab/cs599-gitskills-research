# Final paper requirements

The current PDF and Word file are Checkpoint 1 documents. They are not a completed final paper and should not be submitted as the final manuscript.

The module requires an anonymized IEEE conference paper with at most four pages of main content plus one page of references. Student names and contributions go in a separate Canvas submission note. The final paper must contain a title and abstract; introduction and related work; dataset and method; results answering each question; discussion and threats to validity; a separate ethical-considerations section before the conclusion; conclusion and references. Negative or inconclusive results are acceptable when supported by a sound method.

Before writing final results, obtain the required subset approval, complete and validate the annotation, run the planned comparisons, and interpret uncertainty. Do not fill results sections with invented numbers or treat data-quality counts as answers to the research questions.

Final Canvas materials must include the paper, a replication package link with a fixed commit or release, slides, peer reviews and responses, the dated activity log, and each member's contribution statement. Presentation content should cover motivation, questions, method, strongest findings, implications and limitations. Conference submission is optional.
